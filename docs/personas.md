# Personas

This file defines people to find within the industries in `docs/industries.md`. An industry is an organization type; a persona is a role held by a person.

## sailing_program_leadership

Label: Sailing Program Leadership
Description: Leaders and coaches responsible for club sailing, racing, and junior programs.
Industries: yacht_club

### Qualification notes

- Approve coach titles only when the role belongs to sailing, racing, junior sailing, or a boat-class program.
- Do not qualify fitness, racquet, swim, or unrelated athletic coaches unless the user requests them.

- Sailing Director
- Coach
- Head Coach

- Fleet Coach
