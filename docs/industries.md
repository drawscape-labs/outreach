# Industries

This file defines the organizations to pursue. Personas are defined separately in `docs/personas.md`.

## Strategy

Name: Drawscape
Workspace: Drawscape Outreach
Website: https://www.drawscape.io
Offering: Custom artwork based on a client's aircraft, automobile, sailboat, or yacht.
Value Proposition: Create personalized artwork that sellers and member organizations can give to clients or members after a sale, at an event, or as a recognition gift.
Default Candidate Count: 10

### Minimum evidence

- Verify the account through a current official website or another authoritative public source.
- Require evidence that the account matches at least one configured industry.
- Prefer accounts with visible client-facing teams matching a configured persona.

### Global exclusions

- Service-only or parts-only businesses without a relevant sales motion.
- Rental-only businesses unless the user explicitly requests them.
- Defunct, acquired, or ambiguous businesses whose current identity cannot be verified.

### Discovery sources

- Official company websites and company-controlled team or location pages.
- Manufacturer and brand dealer locators.
- Industry associations and professional directories.
- Public LinkedIn company pages and public search results.
- Reputable industry directories used as discovery leads, followed by primary-source verification.

## Priorities

Default: medium

- high | High | Strong evidence of fit, a repeatable high-value client relationship, and reachable matching contacts.
- medium | Medium | Qualified account with plausible fit, but weaker volume, contact, or positioning evidence.
- low | Low | Adjacent account with limited evidence for the configured value proposition.

## aircraft

Label: Aircraft Dealers & Brokers
Description: Companies that sell or broker business, private, or general-aviation aircraft.
Aliases: aircraft | aviation
Match Terms: aircraft sales | aircraft dealer | aircraft brokerage | aircraft broker | aviation sales
Personas: sales_advisors | brokers | sales_leadership | business_development

### Qualify

- Current evidence of aircraft sales, acquisitions, brokerage, or dealership activity.
- A visible sales, brokerage, business-development, or client-advisory function.

### Exclude

- Maintenance, avionics, charter, flight training, or management businesses with no aircraft sales evidence.

### Priority high

- Established aircraft seller or broker with a visible client-facing sales team and current inventory or transaction evidence.

### Priority medium

- Verified aircraft seller or broker with limited public evidence about volume or team size.

### Priority low

- Aviation business with aircraft sales as a secondary or weakly supported activity.

### Sources

- Aircraft dealer and broker directories.
- Manufacturer sales and authorized-dealer directories.
- Official aircraft inventory and team pages.

### Queries

- "aircraft sales" brokerage team
- "aircraft broker" sales team
- business aircraft dealer sales

## automotive

Aliases: automotive | auto | car | cars
Match Terms: porsche | luxury automobile | luxury automotive | luxury car | car dealership | automotive dealership | auto dealer
Personas: sales_advisors | sales_leadership | dealership_leadership | business_development

- Current evidence of vehicle sales through a dealership or dealer group.

### Queries

## yacht

Label: Yacht & Sailboat Brokers
Description: Brokerages and dealers that sell yachts, sailboats, sailing catamarans, or related high-value vessels.
Aliases: yacht | yachts | boat | boats | sailboat | sailboats
Match Terms: yacht brokerage | yacht broker | yacht sales | sailboat sales | sailing catamaran | sailing yacht
Personas: sales_advisors | brokers | sales_leadership | business_development

### Qualify

- Current evidence of yacht, sailboat, sailing-catamaran, or boat sales and brokerage.
- Prefer accounts with visible brokers, sales advisors, or sales leadership.

### Exclude

- Marinas, charter-only operators, boatyards, repair businesses, or sailing schools without sales or brokerage activity.

### Priority high

- Mainstream or high-volume sailboat dealer or broker with production sailboats, sailing catamarans, active used sailboat inventory, or relevant brands.

### Priority medium

- Powerboat, motor-yacht, general-boat, mixed-yacht, or otherwise ambiguous broker when high or low fit is not clearly proven.

### Priority low

- Primarily luxury-yacht or superyacht broker whose sail evidence is limited to sailing superyachts, charters, or occasional listings.

### Sources

- Official brokerage inventory and broker pages.
- Yacht Brokers Association of America CPYB directory.
- Sailboat manufacturer dealer locators.

### Queries

- "yacht brokerage" brokers team
- "sailboat dealer" sales team
- "yacht broker" sales director

## yacht_club

Label: Yacht Clubs
Description: Private sailing and yachting clubs with member-gift, regatta, event, trophy, or recognition opportunities.
Aliases: yacht club | sailing club | private club
Match Terms: private yacht club | yacht club | sailing club | yacht club regatta | yacht club events
Personas: club_leadership | sailing_program_leadership | event_leadership

### Qualify

- A current private sailing or yachting club with active members, sailing programs, regattas, private events, or recognition programs.

### Exclude

- Commercial marinas, public boat ramps, and social clubs with no meaningful sailing, yachting, event, or recognition activity.

### Priority high

- Private club with active sailing programs, major regattas, member events, and clear gift, trophy, or recognition relevance.

### Priority medium

- Verified private yacht or sailing club with plausible gift or event relevance but limited public program detail.

### Priority low

- Club or marina with weak evidence of private membership, sailing programs, events, or recognition opportunities.

### Sources

- Official club staff, program, and event pages.
- Yacht and sailing club association directories.
- Regatta calendars and member-event pages.

### Queries

- "yacht club" sailing director
- "yacht club" private events
- "sailing club" regatta director
